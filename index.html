I hope you know how much you mean to me. I also know it's literally not possible. You are truly my perfect partner. And when I say that, I don't mean you're perfect. For the coffee shop & the beach blanket

Twelve real questions

Six about the years behind you, six about the years ahead. Take turns answering. Don't rush. The good ones are harder than they look.

Part One

About what's been

For Intelligentsia Coffee · Saturday

  1. Of the places we've lived. Charlottesville, Boston, Redwood City, the frat house in lockdown, Kigali on rotation. Which version of us would you go back to for a week, and what was it about that version?
  2. When did we stop feeling like two people and start feeling like one thing? Was there a moment, or did you just look up one day and notice?
  3. What's something I do that you used to find hard about me. And now find easy, or even love?
  4. What's one of my fears you can see clearly, that I can't see in myself?
  5. What's a small thing I've done that you don't think I know affected you?
  6. If you could send a single sentence back to first-year-us at UVA, what would it say?
Part Two

About what's next

For the beach blanket · Sunday

  1. Picture us five years out. What's a version you're a little afraid of? And one you're genuinely excited about?
  2. What's something you've been quietly hoping I'd notice you wanted me to ask you about? Ask it now.
  3. What's our pattern in a fight that doesn't serve us. And what would replace it? Be specific.
  4. Name one thing we keep deferring that we should decide before September 6.
  5. What's a tradition you want to start in our first year of marriage that you'd still want at fifty?
  6. On a normal Wednesday in year ten, what does our marriage feel like? Not look like. Feel like.
