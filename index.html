Three days at the edge of the country. A studio on Euclid Street, a borrowed car, a long run on the sand, a dress fitting for the wedding that's coming, a quiet table somewhere with handmade pasta and candlelight, a sauna in the morning, the Venice canals at golden hour.

Six years together. One wedding ahead. This weekend sits right in the middle of those two things — the long courtship and the rest of your life. Below is the plan, loose enough to live in, tight enough to keep you fed and on time. Tap any restaurant to call. Cross off the bookings as you go.

Sugarfish · 1345 2nd St · 310.393.3338

Order the Trust Me set for two, add the toro hand roll, a salmon hand roll, and salmon eggs. Pickup is faster than delivery and the shop is six minutes away. Open a bottle while you wait.

Home · Laptop · Candles

No TV in the apartment — cue up the laptop. Three picks worth your evening: John Mulaney: Baby J (Netflix), Nate Bargatze: Hello World (Prime), or Ali Wong: Single Lady (Netflix). Dim the lights. This is the easy part.

Day Two

The Main Event

Saturday23 May
8:00 AM

Long run on the strand

SM Pier → Venice → Marina del Rey

Eight-ish miles if you're feeling it. The path is flat and beautiful and almost entirely yours that early. Bring water.

active
11:00 AM

Brunch & wedding planning — side by side

The Butcher's Daughter · Abbot Kinney · 310.981.3004

Hannah's pick and the right one. Plant-filled, slow-paced, perfect for spreading a notebook on the table. Order the lemon waffles, a matcha, and the avocado toast. Big questions for the notebook: guest list anchors, three words for the day, vows or no vows, must-haves vs. nice-to-haves.

brunch your wedding
12:15 PM

Wander Abbot Kinney

Aviator Nation · Burro · General Store

One of the prettiest stretches in LA. Window-shop, grab a second coffee, let it be aimless. You'll see it again on Sunday.

1:00 PM

Couples massage

Raven Spa · 208 Pier Ave · 310.399.4747

Sixty-minute couples Swedish. Hidden gem on Pier & Main, surprisingly reasonable for the area, post-massage tea and fruit. Book this immediately. Backup: Tikkun Holistic on 4th Street.

reset romantic
3:00 PM

Hit the road for Huntington Beach

405 South · ~75 minutes

Do not leave later than this. Saturday afternoon traffic on the 405 is the one thing that can blow up the day. Play the wedding playlist. Hold hands at the lights.

4:45 PM

The wedding dress fitting ✦

Layah's Bridal Boutique · Huntington Beach · 949.771.6441

The dress. Take pictures. Take notes. Take your time. Josh, this is the only moment of the trip where being five minutes early matters — and probably the only time in your life you'll watch her try on a wedding dress.

the dress ✦
6:00 PM

Drive back along the coast

Huntington Beach → Santa Monica

Traffic should soften by now. You'll be back at the apartment around 7:15 with just enough time to change for dinner.

7:45 PM

See the three options below — Felix recommended

Cobi's was the first pick but they're booked. The three options that follow are better, honestly. Felix is the star — handmade pasta, Michelin Bib Gourmand, and the right kind of warm-and-buzzy that an anniversary deserves.

the dinner anniversary

A beach blanket & the big conversation — before the wedding

Annenberg Beach House · 415 PCH

Wide sand, quieter than the pier, room to spread out. Bring journals, water, something to eat. Six years together. What we love about us. What we want from the wedding day. What we want from being married. No phones for an hour.

the conversation

A long, lazy afternoon

Apartment · Couch · Each other

Venice canals at golden hour

Free · Park near Dell Ave

Hannah's strongest rec. Quiet bridges, lit-up bungalows, water reflecting everything. Walk slowly. It'll take you forty-five minutes if you let it.

golden hour romantic
